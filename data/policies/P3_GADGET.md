# P3 — Gadget Cover Insurance Policy

**Policy ID:** P3_GADGET  
**Policy Number:** NH-GC-2026-00309  
**Insurer:** BrightCircuit Device Assurance Ltd.  
**Jurisdiction:** Commonwealth of Norhaven  
**Currency:** United States Dollar (USD)  
**Annual Premium:** USD 120  
**Policyholder:** The registered owner of the insured device  
**Insured Property:** One or more eligible electronic devices listed in the insurer's device schedule  
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026  
**Expiration Date:** September 30, 2027  

## 1. Policy Overview

### 1.1 Annual Premium

The annual premium for this policy is USD 120 for the coverage described in this document. The premium applies to the insured device schedule confirmed by BrightCircuit Device Assurance Ltd. Coverage is conditional on the device being registered and the policy becoming effective.

### 1.2 Insured Device and Policy Period

An insured device is a portable electronic device identified by type, manufacturer, model, and serial number or another unique identifier in the device schedule. Coverage begins at 12:01 a.m. on October 1, 2026, and ends at 11:59 p.m. on September 30, 2027, local time in the Commonwealth of Norhaven.

A device not listed in the confirmed schedule is not covered merely because it belongs to the policyholder. The policyholder must notify the insurer of any requested device substitution and obtain written confirmation before treating a replacement device as insured.

### 1.3 Currency and Definitions

All monetary amounts are expressed in USD. **Accidental damage** means sudden, unintended physical damage caused by an identifiable external event, such as an accidental drop or liquid spill. **Repair cost** means a reasonable, documented cost to restore an insured device to functional condition. **Total loss** means damage that cannot reasonably be repaired or for which a verified repair estimate exceeds the applicable per-device limit.

### 1.4 Ownership and Use

The policyholder must have a lawful ownership interest in the insured device or be otherwise authorized to insure it. The device must be used for ordinary personal, educational, or professional purposes consistent with its design. Coverage does not automatically extend to devices owned by another person.

## 2. Covered Accidental Damage

### 2.1 Accidental Physical Damage

The policy covers eligible repair costs for sudden and unintended physical damage to an insured device during the policy period. Examples include a cracked screen caused by an accidental drop, damage from an unintended impact, or a liquid spill that directly damages internal components.

The claimant must provide evidence of the incident and the resulting damage. Damage caused by a gradual process, ordinary wear, or a condition that existed before the effective date is not accidental damage under this clause.

### 2.2 Per-Device Coverage Limit

The maximum benefit payable for an insured device is USD 1,500 per device during the policy period. The limit applies to the aggregate benefits paid for that device, not to each separate repair. Multiple claims involving the same device cannot cause total benefits to exceed USD 1,500 for the policy period.

If a repair is possible at a cost below the remaining limit, the insurer may pay the eligible repair cost after application of the deductible. If the device is a verified total loss, the benefit is limited to the lesser of the documented eligible loss and the remaining per-device limit.

### 2.3 Repair Assessment

The insurer may require an inspection, repair estimate, diagnostic report, or assessment by an authorized repair provider. A repair recommendation does not guarantee payment. The insurer must first determine that the damage is accidental, occurred during the policy period, and is not excluded.

### 2.4 Replacement and Parts

When repair is not reasonably possible, the insurer may determine the benefit using the verified eligible loss, subject to the per-device limit and deductible. Any replacement arrangement must be approved by the insurer. The policy does not guarantee a new device of the same model, and a replacement benefit cannot exceed the applicable remaining limit.

## 3. Deductible and Claims

### 3.1 Deductible Per Claim

The policyholder is responsible for a deductible of USD 75 for each approved claim. The deductible is applied to the eligible loss before the insurer calculates its payment. For example, if an eligible repair costs USD 600 and the applicable per-device limit has not been exhausted, the amount payable before any other applicable limitation is USD 525.

A denied claim does not create a benefit payment. The deductible does not increase the per-device limit, and the insurer will not pay more than the eligible loss remaining after the deductible.

### 3.2 Required Evidence of Damage

A claimant must provide reasonable evidence of the incident and the damage, which may include:

- A completed claim form identifying the policy and insured device.
- The device make, model, and serial number or another unique identifier.
- Photographs or video showing the damage, where practical.
- A repair estimate or diagnostic report from a qualified repair provider.
- Proof of ownership or purchase, where reasonably available.
- A description of the date, location, and circumstances of the incident.

The insurer may request the device for inspection when necessary to verify the claim.

### 3.3 Claim Submission Deadline

A claim must be submitted within 30 calendar days after the date the accidental damage occurred. The claimant should report the incident promptly and preserve the device in its damaged condition until the insurer provides instructions, unless immediate action is needed to prevent further damage or protect personal safety.

A claim submitted after the deadline may be denied unless the insurer accepts a documented reason for the delay under its review procedure.

### 3.4 Inspection and Decision

The insurer reviews the incident description, evidence of damage, device registration, applicable deductible, remaining per-device limit, and exclusions. It may arrange an inspection or request an independent repair estimate.

The insurer will provide a written decision identifying the approved amount or the reason for denial. If approved, payment will be made according to the authorized repair or reimbursement process.

## 4. Exclusions

### 4.1 Loss While Left Unattended

Loss of a device while it is left unattended in a public, shared, or unsecured location is excluded. For example, a laptop left unattended on a public table and subsequently missing is not covered under this policy.

This exclusion applies to loss of the device and does not automatically determine whether separately documented physical damage is covered. The claimant must establish that the claimed damage resulted from an eligible accidental event rather than an excluded loss.

### 4.2 Theft and Mysterious Disappearance

Theft, unexplained disappearance, or inability to locate a device without evidence of a covered accidental damage event is excluded. The policy is designed to cover eligible accidental physical damage and does not constitute general theft insurance.

### 4.3 Wear, Cosmetic Deterioration, and Mechanical Failure

Ordinary wear and tear, gradual deterioration, scratches that do not affect function, manufacturer defects, and mechanical or electrical failure without a covered accidental event are excluded. Manufacturer warranty coverage should be pursued for defects that are not caused by accidental damage.

### 4.4 Intentional Damage and Reckless Conduct

Damage intentionally caused by the policyholder or a person acting with the policyholder's consent is excluded. A claim may also be denied to the extent the damage results from deliberate misuse or conduct inconsistent with ordinary device operation.

### 4.5 Pre-existing Damage

Damage that existed before the effective date or before the device was added to the confirmed device schedule is excluded. The insurer may use purchase records, earlier photographs, diagnostic records, or inspection findings to assess whether damage pre-dated coverage.

### 4.6 Unverified Claims and Excess Costs

The insurer may deny amounts that cannot be reasonably supported by repair records or evidence of loss. Costs above the remaining per-device limit, nonessential upgrades, and optional accessories not included in the approved repair are not payable.

## 5. Claims Procedure

### 5.1 Claim Deadline

The claimant must submit the claim within 30 calendar days after the incident, as stated in Clause 3.3. The submission must identify the insured device and include a concise description of the incident and the damage claimed.

### 5.2 Submission Channel

Claims must be submitted through the BrightCircuit Device Assurance Ltd. online claims portal or another channel authorized by the insurer. The claimant will receive a reference number for tracking and should retain a copy of the submission and all supporting evidence.

### 5.3 Review and Additional Information

The insurer may request additional photographs, repair estimates, proof of ownership, device inspection, or clarification about the incident. The claimant must provide reasonable cooperation. A claim cannot be approved solely because a device is damaged; the cause of damage and the applicable policy conditions must also be established.

### 5.4 Settlement and Payment

For an approved claim, the insurer determines the eligible repair or loss amount, applies the USD 75 deductible, and checks the remaining USD 1,500 per-device limit. The insurer will not pay more than the eligible loss or the remaining limit.

Where another policy may cover the same damage, the claimant must disclose it under Clause 5.5 before payment is finalized.

### 5.5 Other Insurance and Overlapping Coverage

The claimant must disclose any other insurance that may cover the same device or incident, including travel insurance or renters insurance. The claimant must identify whether a claim has been submitted to another insurer and whether any payment has been received.

The existence of another policy does not automatically entitle the claimant to full payment under both policies. Each insurer will assess its own coverage terms, applicable limits, deductibles, and the amount of the actual loss. The claimant may not receive total reimbursement exceeding the eligible loss.

### 5.6 Disputed Decisions

A claimant may request a review by identifying the claim reference number, explaining the basis for disagreement, and providing additional relevant evidence. The insurer will record and communicate the result of the review.

## 6. General Conditions

### 6.1 Duty to Prevent Further Damage

After an incident, the policyholder must take reasonable steps to prevent additional damage when safe to do so. Costs of emergency measures may be considered only when reasonable, documented, and directly connected to preventing further covered damage.

### 6.2 Device Changes

A replacement device is not automatically covered when the original device is repaired, replaced, sold, or retired. The insurer must confirm any change to the device schedule in writing.

### 6.3 Governing Jurisdiction

This policy is governed by the fictional insurance framework of the Commonwealth of Norhaven and is intended for simulation, testing, and educational demonstration.

### 6.4 Policy Reference

The policy ID **P3_GADGET** and clause numbers are stable identifiers for claim calculations and policy retrieval. In particular, Clause 3.1 defines the per-claim deductible, Clause 4.1 defines the unattended-loss exclusion, and Clause 5.1 defines the claim deadline.
