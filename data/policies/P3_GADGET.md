# Norland Gadget Cover (fictional)

*Fictional policy written for a competition demo. Not a real insurance product.*

## Part 1. What is covered

**1.1 Covered devices.** Covered devices are the phones, laptops and tablets listed in your policy schedule, bought new within the last 24 months.

**1.2 Personal use only.** Devices used mainly for paid work or business are not covered.

## Part 2. Cover period

**2.1 Cover start date and period.** Cover starts on the cover start date in your policy schedule and runs for 12 months.

## Part 3. Damage

**3.1 Accidental damage.** We repair or replace a covered device damaged by a sudden and unexpected accident, up to USD 1,500 per device. You pay USD 75 for each claim.

**3.2 Liquid damage.** Damage caused by spilled liquids counts as accidental damage.

## Part 4. Theft

**4.1 Theft.** Theft is covered only if the device was locked away or attached to you at the time. Devices left unattended in a public place are not covered.

## Part 5. Exclusions and travel

**5.1 Exclusions.** We do not cover cosmetic damage, wear and tear, or damage caused by deliberate acts.

**5.2 Travel.** Cover applies worldwide for trips of up to 60 days. After that, cover is suspended until you return to Norland.

## Part 6. Claims

**6.1 Claim deadline.** You must report damage within 30 days of the incident and provide the repair estimate or receipt.

**6.2 Documents.** For theft you must also provide a police report number.

**7.1 Other insurance.** If another policy covers the same loss, we pay only our share after the other insurer's payment, and the total paid by all insurers cannot exceed the loss.
