# Norland Student Health Plan (fictional)

*Fictional policy written for a competition demo. Not a real insurance product.*

## Part 1. About this plan

**1.1 Who is covered.** This plan covers the enrolled international student named in the policy schedule while studying in Norland. It does not cover family members.

**1.2 Cover start date.** Cover starts on the date shown in your policy schedule (the cover start date). Costs from before that date are not covered.

## Part 2. Limits

**2.1 Annual limit.** The maximum we pay in any policy year is USD 50,000 for all covered benefits combined.

**2.2 Policy year.** A policy year runs for 12 months from the cover start date. Limits and deductibles reset at the start of each policy year.

## Part 3. What you pay

**3.1 Deductible.** You pay the first USD 300 of covered outpatient and inpatient costs in each policy year before we pay.

**3.2 Co-insurance.** Where this plan says we pay a percentage, you pay the rest.

## Part 4. Medical care

**4.1 Inpatient care.** After the deductible we pay 100% of covered inpatient costs. Room and board is limited to USD 200 per night.

**4.2 Outpatient care.** After the deductible we pay 80% of covered outpatient costs, up to USD 2,000 per policy year.

**4.3 Emergency room.** Emergency room visits are covered as outpatient care unless they lead to admission, in which case they are covered as inpatient care.

## Part 5. Waiting periods

**5.1 Non-emergency admission.** Planned inpatient admissions in the first 30 days after the cover start date are not covered. This waiting period does not apply to emergencies.

## Part 6. Dental

**6.1 Dental.** Only emergency dental treatment after an accident or acute pain is covered, up to USD 500 per policy year. Routine cleaning, fillings and orthodontics are not covered.

**6.2 Waiting period for dental.** Dental benefits begin 90 days after your cover start date. This waiting period does not apply to dental treatment needed because of an accident.

## Part 7. Exclusions

**7.1 General exclusions.** We do not pay for conditions that existed before your cover start date, for injuries from extreme sports, or for cosmetic treatment.

**7.2 Extreme sports.** Extreme sports include motor racing, skydiving and free climbing.

**7.3 Treatment abroad.** Treatment received outside Norland is not covered by this plan. Please check your travel policy.

## Part 8. Claims

**8.1 Claim deadline.** You must submit a claim within 60 days of the treatment date.

**8.2 Documents.** Send the itemised bill, proof of payment and a doctor's note.

**8.3 Other insurance.** If another policy also covers the same cost, we pay only what remains after the other insurer's payment.
