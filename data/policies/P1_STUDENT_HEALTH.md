# P1 — Student Health Insurance Policy

**Policy ID:** P1_HEALTH  
**Policy Number:** NH-SH-2026-00184  
**Insurer:** Northstar Student Assurance Ltd.  
**Jurisdiction:** Commonwealth of Norhaven  
**Currency:** United States Dollar (USD)  
**Annual Premium:** USD 720  
**Policyholder:** Norhaven University Student Services  
**Insured Person:** An eligible student registered under the policyholder's student enrollment program  
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026  
**Expiration Date:** September 30, 2027  

## 1. Policy Overview

### 1.1 Annual Premium

The annual premium for this policy is USD 720 per insured student for a twelve-month coverage period. The premium must be paid in accordance with the enrollment agreement issued by Northstar Student Assurance Ltd. Coverage is subject to the policy becoming effective and the insured person meeting the eligibility requirements described herein.

### 1.2 Policy Period

Coverage begins at 12:01 a.m. on October 1, 2026, and ends at 11:59 p.m. on September 30, 2027, local time in the Commonwealth of Norhaven. An expense must arise from a covered event during this period to qualify for benefits, unless an applicable provision expressly states otherwise.

### 1.3 Eligibility

An insured person must be an enrolled student recognized by the policyholder and listed in the insurer's enrollment records. Coverage applies only to the named or registered insured person. Benefits may not be transferred to another student or family member.

### 1.4 Currency and Interpretation

All monetary limits, deductibles, premiums, and benefit calculations under this policy are expressed in USD. If an eligible expense is incurred in another currency, the insurer will convert it into USD using the conversion rate documented by the insurer on the date the expense was incurred.

### 1.5 Definitions

For this policy, an **eligible expense** is a medically necessary charge for a covered service, supported by appropriate records and incurred during the policy period. A **policy year** is the twelve-month period beginning on the effective date or its anniversary. A **medically necessary service** is a service reasonably required to evaluate or treat an illness or injury and provided by a qualified healthcare professional.

## 2. Covered Medical Benefits

### 2.1 Inpatient Hospitalization

The policy covers eligible inpatient hospitalization expenses for medically necessary treatment of an illness or injury, subject to the annual coverage limit, deductible, room sub-limit, and exclusions stated in this document. Covered expenses may include hospital facility charges, medically necessary procedures, prescribed inpatient medication, and professional treatment directly related to the admission.

For eligible inpatient expenses, the policy pays 100% of covered expenses remaining after application of the applicable annual deductible, subject to all benefit limits. This provision does not mean that expenses exceeding a sub-limit or the annual coverage limit will be reimbursed.

### 2.2 Hospital Room Sub-limit

Eligible standard hospital room charges are limited to USD 200 per night. If the insured person chooses a room costing more than USD 200 per night, the amount above the sub-limit is the insured person's responsibility and is not treated as an eligible expense for reimbursement.

The room sub-limit applies to the room charge only. Other eligible inpatient expenses remain subject to the terms of Clause 2.1 and the overall annual limit.

### 2.3 Outpatient Treatment

The policy covers eligible outpatient consultations, diagnostic services, and treatment that do not require an overnight hospital admission. The insurer pays 80% of eligible outpatient expenses after application of the annual deductible, where applicable.

The insured person is responsible for the remaining 20% of eligible outpatient expenses, as well as any amount exceeding the outpatient cap or excluded by another clause. The outpatient benefit is subject to the annual cap described in Clause 2.4.

### 2.4 Outpatient Annual Cap

The maximum amount payable for outpatient benefits is USD 2,000 per policy year. This cap represents the insurer's maximum aggregate payment for eligible outpatient services during that policy year, not the total amount the insured person may spend on outpatient care.

Once the insurer has paid USD 2,000 in outpatient benefits during the policy year, no further outpatient benefit is payable for that year. The cap does not increase the overall annual coverage limit.

### 2.5 Emergency Dental Treatment

Emergency dental treatment is covered up to USD 500 per policy year when required to address sudden pain, accidental dental injury, or another urgent dental condition requiring prompt treatment. Routine cleaning, elective cosmetic procedures, orthodontic treatment, and planned dental work are not emergency treatment under this policy.

Emergency dental benefits are subject to the waiting period in Clause 3.3, the annual coverage limit, and the exclusions in Section 4.

## 3. Deductible and Coverage Limits

### 3.1 Annual Deductible

The insured person is responsible for the first USD 300 of eligible expenses subject to the annual deductible during each policy year. The deductible is applied once to eligible expenses accumulated during that policy year. After the deductible has been satisfied, benefits are calculated according to the applicable coverage percentage and sub-limits.

Expenses that are excluded or exceed an applicable sub-limit do not count toward satisfying the deductible. The insurer will maintain a record of deductible amounts applied to processed claims.

### 3.2 Overall Annual Coverage Limit

The maximum aggregate benefit payable under this policy is USD 50,000 per insured person per policy year. All benefits paid under inpatient, outpatient, and emergency dental coverage count toward this overall limit unless a benefit is expressly identified as outside the limit.

When total benefits paid reach USD 50,000, the insurer will not pay additional benefits for that policy year. The annual limit does not override a lower benefit-specific cap, sub-limit, deductible, waiting period, or exclusion.

### 3.3 Dental Waiting Period

Emergency dental benefits are subject to a waiting period of 90 calendar days beginning on the effective date of coverage. A dental event occurring before completion of the waiting period is not eligible for emergency dental reimbursement, even if treatment occurs after the waiting period.

The waiting period applies only to the emergency dental benefit. It does not independently impose a 90-day waiting period on inpatient or outpatient medical benefits.

### 3.4 Benefit Calculation Order

For a covered claim, the insurer first determines whether the event and expenses qualify under the policy. It then removes excluded charges and amounts above applicable sub-limits, applies the remaining annual deductible where applicable, calculates the benefit percentage, and checks the remaining annual and benefit-specific limits. The insurer will not pay more than the eligible amount or the remaining applicable limit.

## 4. Exclusions and Limitations

### 4.1 Congenital Conditions

Expenses arising directly from the diagnosis or treatment of a congenital condition are excluded under this policy. A condition is congenital when it exists at birth or arises from a developmental abnormality present from birth. The exclusion applies to expenses directly attributable to the congenital condition; it does not automatically exclude unrelated treatment for a separate covered illness or injury.

### 4.2 Extreme Sports

Treatment for an injury sustained while participating in extreme sports is excluded. Extreme sports include activities designated by the insurer as presenting an unusually high risk of serious injury, including base jumping, competitive cliff diving, and unprotected high-altitude climbing. Ordinary recreational exercise is not automatically classified as an extreme sport.

### 4.3 Non-Medically Necessary Services

The policy does not cover services that are not medically necessary, services provided solely for cosmetic purposes, or treatment unsupported by appropriate clinical records. The insurer may request additional documentation to determine medical necessity.

### 4.4 Uncovered Charges

The policy does not cover administrative penalties, late-payment fees, personal convenience purchases, or charges that are not part of an eligible medical service. Any expense excluded under this section remains the insured person's responsibility.

## 5. Claims Procedure

### 5.1 Claim Submission Deadline

A claim must be submitted within 60 calendar days after the date of the covered medical event or the date the relevant treatment expense was incurred, whichever is later. A claim submitted after this deadline may be denied unless the insurer determines that a documented circumstance prevented timely submission and the delay is accepted under its claims review procedure.

### 5.2 Required Documents

The insured person must provide the following documents where applicable:

- A completed claim form identifying the insured person, policy ID, date of treatment, and amount claimed.
- Itemized invoices from the hospital, clinic, dentist, or healthcare provider.
- Proof of payment for expenses already paid by the insured person.
- Medical records, diagnostic results, prescriptions, or discharge summaries supporting the treatment.
- Bank or payment details needed to issue an approved reimbursement.

The insurer may request additional records reasonably necessary to verify eligibility, medical necessity, or the amount claimed.

### 5.3 Submission and Review

Claims must be submitted through the insurer's designated online claims portal or another submission channel authorized in writing. The claimant must retain copies of all submitted documents and the claim reference number.

After receiving a claim, the insurer checks policy eligibility, the event date, the documents provided, applicable limits, and the remaining deductible. If information is missing, the insurer may request clarification before completing its assessment.

### 5.4 Claim Decision and Payment

The insurer will issue a decision identifying the approved amount, the amount denied, and the policy clauses used to determine the result. An approved benefit will be paid to the insured person or an authorized healthcare provider using the payment method recorded in the claim.

A payment does not increase any annual limit or restore deductible amounts already applied during the policy year.

### 5.5 Review of a Denied Claim

The insured person may request a review of a denied or partially approved claim by submitting a written explanation and any additional supporting documents. The request should identify the claim reference number and the disputed decision. The insurer will review the submitted information and provide a written outcome.

## 6. General Conditions

### 6.1 Accuracy of Information

The policyholder and insured person must provide accurate enrollment and claim information. Material misrepresentation may result in a claim being denied to the extent permitted by the policy and applicable law in the Commonwealth of Norhaven.

### 6.2 No Guarantee of Payment

Possession of this policy does not guarantee payment of every medical expense. Each claim remains subject to eligibility, medical necessity, exclusions, deductibles, coverage percentages, waiting periods, and applicable limits.

### 6.3 Governing Jurisdiction

This policy is governed by the fictional insurance framework of the Commonwealth of Norhaven. Any interpretation for this project is intended solely for simulation, testing, and educational demonstration.

### 6.4 Policy Reference

The policy ID **P1_HEALTH** and the clause numbers in this document are stable references for claim processing, policy retrieval, and coverage explanation. Any later revision must preserve these identifiers or update all systems that reference them.
