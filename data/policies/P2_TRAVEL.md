# P2 — Travel Insurance Policy

**Policy ID:** P2_TRAVEL  
**Policy Number:** NH-TR-2026-00217  
**Insurer:** Meridian Passage Insurance Co.  
**Jurisdiction:** Commonwealth of Norhaven  
**Currency:** United States Dollar (USD)  
**Annual Premium:** USD 180  
**Policyholder:** Norhaven University Travel Office  
**Insured Person:** An eligible student or staff member listed on an approved travel enrollment record  
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026  
**Expiration Date:** September 30, 2027  

## 1. Policy Overview

### 1.1 Annual Premium

The annual premium for this policy is USD 180 per insured person. The premium provides the benefits described in this document for eligible travel events occurring during the policy period. Coverage is subject to enrollment confirmation and payment of the required premium.

### 1.2 Policy Period and Covered Travel

Coverage begins at 12:01 a.m. on October 1, 2026, and ends at 11:59 p.m. on September 30, 2027, local time in the Commonwealth of Norhaven. A covered travel event must occur during an eligible trip within this policy period.

A covered trip is a temporary journey away from the insured person's normal place of residence. Travel must be undertaken for personal, educational, or authorized institutional purposes. The insured person must retain reasonable evidence of the trip, such as a booking confirmation, itinerary, or travel authorization.

### 1.3 Currency

All premiums, deductibles, limits, and benefits are stated in USD. Expenses paid in another currency will be converted into USD using a documented exchange rate applicable to the date of expense or another consistently applied rate recorded by the insurer.

### 1.4 Definitions

**Overseas medical expense** means an eligible medical expense incurred outside the insured person's country of normal residence during a covered trip. **Baggage** means personal belongings carried by the insured person for use during travel. **Electronic item** means a portable electronic device, such as a laptop, tablet, phone, camera, or similar personal device.

## 2. Covered Travel Benefits

### 2.1 Overseas Emergency Medical Expenses

The policy covers eligible emergency medical expenses incurred outside the insured person's country of normal residence during a covered trip, up to a maximum of USD 100,000 per insured person per policy year.

Covered expenses may include emergency physician services, hospital treatment, diagnostic testing, medically necessary medication, and emergency treatment following an accidental injury or sudden illness. Treatment must be provided by a qualified healthcare professional and supported by itemized documentation.

Routine checkups, elective treatment, and services that can reasonably be postponed until the insured person returns home are not treated as emergency medical expenses under this clause.

### 2.2 Medical Deductible

The insured person is responsible for the first USD 100 of eligible overseas medical expenses subject to this benefit. After the deductible has been satisfied, the insurer may pay eligible remaining expenses up to the applicable limit, subject to exclusions and verification of the claim.

The deductible applies to overseas medical expenses and does not automatically apply to the baggage benefit unless expressly stated in a later written endorsement.

### 2.3 Baggage Loss or Damage

The policy covers eligible loss of, or physical damage to, personal baggage during a covered trip, up to USD 1,000 per insured person per policy year. The claimant must provide reasonable evidence that the baggage was in the insured person's possession or under the responsibility of a travel carrier or accommodation provider at the time of the incident.

For carrier-related incidents, the claimant should obtain a written incident report from the carrier or other responsible party. The insurer may deduct any compensation already received for the same loss when determining the payable amount.

### 2.4 Electronic Item Sub-limit

Within the baggage benefit, eligible loss of or physical damage to electronic items is limited to USD 500 per insured person per policy year. This sub-limit forms part of, and does not add to, the USD 1,000 baggage limit.

The insured person must provide a description of the electronic item, evidence of ownership where reasonably available, and evidence of the damage or loss. An item claimed under another policy must be disclosed as described in Clause 5.3.

### 2.5 Reasonable Loss Documentation

The insurer may request receipts, travel records, carrier reports, photographs, repair estimates, or other documents reasonably necessary to establish the nature and value of a claimed loss. If the original purchase receipt is unavailable, alternative evidence may be considered, but approval is not guaranteed.

### 2.6 Specific Gadget Policy Coordination

Physical damage to an electronic device listed under an active, separate gadget insurance policy is not covered under the baggage electronic-item benefit of this policy. The insured person must disclose the separate policy when submitting a claim.

This clause does not automatically exclude unrelated baggage losses or eligible overseas medical expenses. Claims involving other types of loss remain subject to the applicable terms, limits, deductibles, and exclusions of this policy.

The existence of separate gadget insurance does not guarantee that the gadget insurer will approve a claim. Each insurer must assess the incident under its own policy terms, and the insured person may not receive total reimbursement exceeding the eligible loss.



## 3. Deductibles and Benefit Limits

### 3.1 Medical Deductible Application

The USD 100 deductible is applied to eligible overseas medical expenses before calculating the medical benefit payable. Expenses excluded under this policy or amounts above the applicable limit are not payable merely because the deductible has been satisfied.

### 3.2 Medical Annual Limit

The maximum aggregate payment for eligible overseas medical expenses is USD 100,000 per insured person per policy year. The insurer will not pay more than the eligible expense amount remaining after the deductible or more than the remaining medical limit.

### 3.3 Baggage and Electronics Limits

Baggage benefits are limited to USD 1,000 per policy year, and electronic items are limited to USD 500 within that baggage limit. The electronic sub-limit is not a separate additional benefit. If the baggage limit has been exhausted, no further baggage payment is available during that policy year.

### 3.4 Benefit Calculation Order

The insurer verifies whether the trip and event are covered, determines the eligible loss, applies the relevant deductible where applicable, checks the baggage or medical sub-limit, and then calculates the amount payable. Compensation already received from a carrier or another insurer for the same loss may be considered to prevent duplicate reimbursement of the same expense.

## 4. Exclusions

### 4.1 Motorcycle Accidents Without a Valid License

Medical expenses, baggage losses, or other claimed losses arising directly from an accident while the insured person operates a motorcycle without a valid license required for that vehicle in the location of operation are excluded. The claimant must provide licensing information when reasonably requested to assess a motorcycle-related claim.

### 4.2 Intentional or Fraudulent Acts

Losses intentionally caused by the insured person, or claims supported by knowingly false documents or material misrepresentations, are excluded to the extent attributable to the intentional or fraudulent conduct.

### 4.3 Unattended Personal Property

Baggage or electronic items left unattended in a public or unsecured location are excluded when the loss results from leaving the item without reasonable supervision. This clause concerns baggage coverage and does not independently determine coverage under a separate gadget policy.

### 4.4 Unverified or Ineligible Expenses

Expenses that cannot be reasonably connected to a covered trip or supported by appropriate records may be denied. Personal purchases unrelated to a covered medical event or baggage loss are not eligible benefits.

### 4.5 Loss Above Applicable Limits

The insured person remains responsible for any expense or loss above the applicable medical, baggage, or electronic-item limit. An exclusion or sub-limit cannot be overridden by the general coverage description.

## 5. Claims Procedure

### 5.1 Claim Submission Deadline

A claim must be submitted within 30 calendar days after the date of the covered event. The claim should include the date and location of the incident, the type of benefit requested, and the amount claimed. Late claims may be denied unless a documented circumstance preventing timely submission is accepted by the insurer under its review procedure.

### 5.2 Required Documents

Depending on the benefit, the claimant must provide:

- A completed claim form and policy ID.
- Travel itinerary, booking confirmation, or institutional travel authorization.
- Medical bills, treatment records, prescriptions, and proof of payment for medical claims.
- Baggage receipts or reasonable evidence of ownership and value.
- Carrier or accommodation incident reports for baggage-related losses, where available.
- Photographs, repair estimates, or other evidence of physical damage.
- Details of compensation or reimbursement received from another party or insurer.

The insurer may request additional evidence reasonably necessary to verify the claim.

### 5.3 Disclosure of Other Insurance

The claimant must disclose any other insurance policy that may cover the same event or expense, including a separate gadget policy or renters policy. The claimant must identify any claim already submitted to another insurer and any amount already paid.

Disclosure does not automatically mean that this policy will pay the full remaining amount. The insurer will assess the applicable terms, limits, and evidence of actual loss before determining the benefit.

### 5.4 Submission and Assessment

Claims must be submitted through the insurer's designated online portal or another authorized submission channel. The claimant should retain the claim reference number and copies of all documents.

The insurer will review trip eligibility, event details, the relevant benefit, deductible, sub-limit, exclusions, and other insurance information before issuing a decision.

### 5.5 Decision and Payment

The insurer will issue a written decision stating the approved amount, any denied amount, and the policy clauses relied upon. Payment is limited to the amount determined under the policy and will be made to the insured person or another authorized recipient.

### 5.6 Review Request

The insured person may request review of a denied or partially approved claim by identifying the claim reference number, explaining the disputed decision, and supplying relevant additional evidence. The insurer will document the result of the review.

## 6. General Conditions

### 6.1 Duty to Cooperate

The insured person must cooperate with reasonable requests for documents or clarification relating to a claim. Failure to provide information necessary to assess the claim may delay or prevent approval.

### 6.2 No Double Recovery

The insured person may not recover more than the actual eligible loss for the same expense. Payments from another insurer, travel carrier, or responsible party may be considered when calculating the amount payable under this policy.

### 6.3 Governing Jurisdiction

This policy is governed by the fictional insurance framework of the Commonwealth of Norhaven and is intended for simulation, testing, and educational demonstration.

### 6.4 Policy Reference

The policy ID **P2_TRAVEL** and clause numbers are stable identifiers for policy retrieval, claim assessment, and coordination with other coverage documents. Changes to these identifiers must be reflected in connected test data and references.
