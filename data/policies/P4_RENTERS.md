# P4 — Renters Insurance Policy

**Policy ID:** P4_RENTERS  
**Policy Number:** NH-RI-2026-00412  
**Insurer:** HavenKey Residential Assurance Co.  
**Jurisdiction:** Commonwealth of Norhaven  
**Currency:** United States Dollar (USD)  
**Annual Premium:** USD 96  
**Policyholder:** The named tenant listed in the policy schedule  
**Insured Premises:** The rented residential unit identified in the policyholder's schedule  
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026  
**Expiration Date:** September 30, 2027  

## 1. Policy Overview

### 1.1 Annual Premium

The annual premium for this policy is USD 96 for the base renters coverage described in this document. Any optional rider must be recorded in the policy schedule and may carry an additional premium. The base premium does not automatically purchase optional benefits that are not listed as active.

### 1.2 Policy Period and Insured Premises

Coverage begins at 12:01 a.m. on October 1, 2026, and ends at 11:59 p.m. on September 30, 2027, local time in the Commonwealth of Norhaven. Coverage applies to the residential premises listed in the confirmed policy schedule and to eligible personal property located there, subject to the terms of this policy.

A change of residence must be reported to the insurer. A new address is not covered until the insurer confirms the change in writing.

### 1.3 Definitions

**Contents** means movable personal property owned by the insured person and ordinarily kept at the insured premises. **Personal liability** means the insured person's legal responsibility for covered bodily injury to another person or physical damage to another person's property. **Rider** means an optional coverage extension that applies only when expressly selected and recorded in the policy schedule.

### 1.4 Currency

All monetary amounts are expressed in USD. Expenses incurred in another currency will be converted into USD using a documented exchange rate applied consistently by the insurer.

## 2. Contents and Personal Liability Coverage

### 2.1 Household Contents

The policy covers eligible accidental physical loss of or damage to household contents at the insured premises, up to an aggregate limit of USD 5,000 per policy year. Eligible contents may include furniture, clothing, books, kitchen equipment, and ordinary personal belongings owned by the insured person.

The claimant must demonstrate ownership or a lawful interest in the property and provide reasonable evidence of its value and the circumstances of the loss. The policy does not cover property belonging to a landlord or another person unless the insured person has an eligible financial interest and the insurer confirms coverage.

### 2.2 Personal Liability

The policy covers the insured person's legal liability for eligible accidental bodily injury to another person or accidental physical damage to another person's property arising from ordinary personal activities at the insured premises or in circumstances specified in the policy schedule.

The maximum aggregate benefit for personal liability is USD 20,000 per policy year. The limit includes covered amounts for which the insured person is legally responsible, subject to exclusions, verification, and any applicable deductible stated in the policy schedule.

### 2.3 Reasonable Evidence of Contents Loss

A contents claim must identify the damaged or missing item, its approximate age and value, and the circumstances of the incident. Supporting evidence may include purchase receipts, photographs, repair estimates, inventory records, or other reasonable proof of ownership and value.

For a loss involving several items, the insurer may assess each item separately before applying the aggregate contents limit.

### 2.4 Liability Documentation

A liability claim must include a description of the incident, the identity of the claimant where available, evidence of injury or property damage, and any demand for compensation received by the insured person. The insurer may request relevant correspondence, repair estimates, medical invoices, or other documents needed to evaluate the alleged liability.

The insured person must not admit liability or agree to a settlement on the insurer's behalf without written authorization.

## 3. Deductible and Limits

### 3.1 Contents Deductible

The insured person is responsible for a deductible of USD 250 for each covered contents claim. The insurer applies the deductible to the eligible contents loss before calculating payment. If the eligible loss does not exceed USD 250, no contents benefit is payable.

The deductible does not increase the USD 5,000 aggregate contents limit. Amounts excluded from coverage or above an applicable limit remain the insured person's responsibility.

### 3.2 Contents Aggregate Limit

The maximum aggregate benefit payable for covered household contents is USD 5,000 per policy year. Payments for multiple contents claims are accumulated toward this limit. Once the limit has been exhausted, no additional contents benefit is payable for the remainder of the policy year.

### 3.3 Personal Liability Limit

The maximum aggregate benefit for covered personal liability is USD 20,000 per policy year. The insurer will not pay more than the insured person's covered legal liability or the remaining limit, whichever is lower. The limit does not imply that every demand for compensation is covered.

### 3.4 Limit Application

For each claim, the insurer determines coverage, verifies the eligible loss or liability, applies the relevant deductible where specified, and checks the remaining applicable limit. Payments under the contents benefit do not automatically reduce the separate personal liability limit, and liability payments do not automatically reduce the contents limit.

## 4. Exclusions and Limitations

### 4.1 Intentional Damage

Loss or damage intentionally caused by the insured person or with the insured person's consent is excluded. The insurer may deny the portion of a claim attributable to intentional conduct.

### 4.2 Ordinary Wear and Tear

Gradual deterioration, ordinary wear and tear, maintenance costs, and mechanical failure without a covered accidental event are excluded. The policy is not a maintenance contract or a manufacturer's warranty.

### 4.3 Unverified Ownership or Value

A contents claim may be denied or reduced if the claimant cannot reasonably establish ownership, the existence of the item, or the amount of the claimed loss. The insurer may consider alternative evidence when an original receipt is unavailable.

### 4.4 Business Property

Property used primarily for commercial operations is excluded unless the insurer has expressly accepted it in writing. Ordinary personal study equipment is not automatically classified as business property solely because it is used for education.

### 4.5 Uncovered Liability

Liability arising from intentional injury, contractual obligations beyond ordinary legal responsibility, or activities expressly excluded by the policy is not covered. A demand or lawsuit alone does not establish that the liability benefit is payable.

## 5. Optional Personal Electronics Rider

### 5.1 Rider Availability and Additional Premium

The policyholder may request an optional personal electronics rider for eligible portable electronic devices. The additional annual premium for this rider is USD 60, payable in addition to the USD 96 base annual premium. The rider applies only when the insurer confirms in writing that it has been selected and identifies the covered devices or device category in the policy schedule.

The rider's additional premium is separate from the base premium and does not change the base contents or liability limits.

### 5.2 Rider Coverage

When active, the rider may cover eligible accidental physical damage to a listed personal electronic device used primarily by the insured person at the insured premises or during ordinary personal activities. Coverage is limited to the terms and device schedule confirmed by the insurer.

The rider does not automatically cover theft, unexplained disappearance, or a device that is not listed or otherwise expressly accepted. The policyholder must submit evidence of the incident and damage.

### 5.3 Coordination With Separate Gadget Insurance

A device insured under this rider may also be insured under a separate gadget policy, including P3_GADGET. The policyholder must disclose the other policy and identify any claim submitted or payment received for the same incident.

This rider does not guarantee a separate full payment when another policy covers the same loss. The insurer will assess the eligible loss, the rider's terms, any applicable deductible and limit, and amounts paid by another insurer. The combined reimbursement must not exceed the eligible loss.

For claim-processing purposes, the existence of P3_GADGET must be treated as a potential coverage overlap, not as automatic proof that both policies will pay.

### 5.4 Rider Limits and Deductible

Any device-specific limit or deductible applicable to the rider must be stated in the written rider schedule issued by the insurer. If the schedule does not specify a separate amount, the insurer must clarify the applicable term before the rider is treated as providing a calculable benefit. The USD 5,000 contents limit must not be interpreted as automatically creating an additional electronics limit.

### 5.5 Rider Claims

A rider claim must identify the device, the incident date, the damage, the repair estimate or documented loss, and any other policy that may cover the same event. The insurer may request inspection or additional records before making a decision.

## 6. Claims Procedure

### 6.1 Claim Notification

The insured person must notify the insurer as soon as reasonably practicable after discovering a covered contents loss, liability incident, or rider-related event. The notification should identify the policy number, incident date, location, and general nature of the claim.

### 6.2 Required Documents

Depending on the type of claim, the insurer may require:

- A completed claim form and policy number.
- A description of the incident and a list of damaged or lost property.
- Proof of ownership and reasonable evidence of value.
- Photographs, repair estimates, or inspection reports.
- Medical or repair records supporting a liability claim.
- Relevant communications, demands, or incident reports.
- Details of other insurance that may cover the same loss.

The claimant must provide additional reasonable information when requested.

### 6.3 Assessment and Decision

The insurer will assess the facts, confirm whether the relevant coverage is active, identify applicable exclusions, calculate eligible loss or liability, apply the relevant deductible and limit, and consider other insurance where appropriate.

The written decision will identify the approved amount or explain the reason for denial. Approval under one benefit does not establish coverage under another benefit.

### 6.4 Payment and Disputes

Approved payments will be made to the insured person or another authorized recipient. If the insured person disputes a decision, the insured person may submit a written review request with the policy number, claim reference, explanation of the disagreement, and supporting evidence.

## 7. General Conditions

### 7.1 Reasonable Care

The insured person must take reasonable steps to protect the insured premises and personal property from avoidable loss. This requirement does not guarantee coverage for an event that is otherwise excluded.

### 7.2 Changes to the Premises or Property

The insured person must notify the insurer of material changes to the insured address or any requested addition to the rider schedule. A requested change becomes effective only when confirmed by the insurer in writing.

### 7.3 Governing Jurisdiction

This policy is governed by the fictional insurance framework of the Commonwealth of Norhaven and is intended for simulation, testing, and educational demonstration.

### 7.4 Policy Reference

The policy ID **P4_RENTERS** and the clause numbers in this document are stable references for retrieval, claim assessment, and comparison with other policies. Any change to rider limits or deductibles must be reflected in the policy schedule and connected test data.
